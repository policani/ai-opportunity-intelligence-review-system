# Opportunity Architecture Brief

Use this structure:

## Recommendation

Route: Prototype / Build / Buy / Automate / Hire-Upskill / Wait / Process-First / Decompose Further / Stop

## Concept Summary

- Business problem
- Workflow
- Users
- Proposed AI role

## Intelligence Stack

- Purpose
- Sensing
- Interpretation
- Decision
- Orchestration
- Learning
- Govern and assure

## Rationale

- Workflow shape
- Value evidence
- True-cost and workflow-shape gate result
- Data readiness
- Risk and control needs
- Portfolio fit

## AI True-Cost And Workflow-Shape Gate

- Workflow trigger, inputs, outputs, exceptions, downstream user, and support owner
- Human review, escalation, error bounds, and consequence of error
- Data permissions and control limits
- Token, model, vendor, license, connector, integration, retrieval, evaluation, monitoring, and support cost drivers
- Cheapest credible proof and nearest non-AI alternative
- Cost uncertainty and unsupported benefit claims

## Proof Plan

- Cheapest credible proof
- Success measure
- Measurement contract: expected outcome, benefit type, metric, baseline, target, actual if available, measurement period, source, measure owner, review cadence, validation need, confidence, realization risk, finance-sensitive flag, downstream route
- Stop condition
- Decision owner

## Human Decision Needed

Name the decision humans must make. Do not make it for them.
